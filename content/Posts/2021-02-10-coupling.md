In the [post on Analyzing Markov Chains using Coupling](/Posts/2020-12-07-markov_chain_analysis_by_coupling), we saw a way to analyze how quickly a Markov Chain mixes by bounding the expected time taken by two arbitrary states to coalesce. This was possible because of a magical theorem that relates the two. Here we'll see how we build up to that theorem. 

As part of an MCMC reading group at IIITH during Spring 2021, I presented Coupling and Path Coupling as tools to analyze the mixing times of Markov Chains. These notes (based on Markov Chains and Mixing Times by Peres et. al.) I describe coupling, an application and some proofs that lead to the afforementioned theorems.
